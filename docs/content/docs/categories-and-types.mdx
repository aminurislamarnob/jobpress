JobPress sorts jobs two ways. **Job categories** are usually your teams or
departments: Engineering, Design, Sales. **Job types** describe the contract:
Full Time, Part Time, Remote, Internship. A job can have several of each.

Both power the category and type filters on the [Jobs Page](/docs/jobs-page),
and categories group the jobs in [Designs V2 and V4](/docs/listing-designs).

## Job categories

![The job categories screen](/docs/images/job-categories.jpg)

Go to **JobPress → Job Categories**. The left side adds a category, and the list
on the right shows every category with its job **Count**.

- **Name** - shown on your site.
- **Slug** - the URL-friendly name. Use it in the
  [shortcode](/docs/shortcode)'s `category` attribute.
- **Parent Job Category** - nest a category under another.
- **Description** - shown under the category name in the grouped designs, so
  write one line that sells the team: "Build and run the products our customers
  rely on every day."

Click **Add New Job Category** to save. Hover a category in the list to
**Edit**, **Quick Edit**, **Delete**, or **View** it.

## Job types

![The job types screen](/docs/images/job-types.jpg)

**JobPress → Job Types** works the same way, with **Add New Job Type**. Types
appear on job cards (**Job Type: Full Time**) and in the job page's summary.

## Assigning them to a job

In the job editor sidebar, open **Job Categories** or **Job Types** and tick the
ones that apply. You can also add a new one from there.

## Category and type pages

![The Engineering category page](/docs/images/category-archive.jpg)

Every category and type gets its own page, such as
`/jobpress_category/engineering/`. It looks like the
[Jobs Page](/docs/jobs-page) with that category already selected in the filter.
Link to it from a team page to show only that team's openings.

<Callout title="Jobs without a category">
In the grouped designs, jobs with no category are listed under
**Other openings**.
</Callout>
