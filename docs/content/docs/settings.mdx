Go to **JobPress → Settings**. The settings screen has three tabs - **General**,
**Appearance**, and **Shortcodes** - each with its own **Save Changes** button.
The **Settings** link under JobPress on the **Plugins** screen opens it too.

## General

![The General settings tab](/docs/images/settings-general.jpg)

- **Single Page Sidebar Position** - tick to show the
  [job page](/docs/single-job) sidebar on the left. It is on the right by
  default.
- **Resume Submit Instruction** - how to apply, used by every job set to
  **Default** and by jobs with no application medium chosen. Email addresses
  become links. For example: "Send your CV and a short cover letter to
  careers@yourcompany.com with the job title in the subject line."
- **Jobs Page** - which page is your [Jobs Page](/docs/jobs-page). JobPress
  creates and selects **Jobs Listing** on activation.
- **Jobs Per Page** - how many jobs the Jobs Page and the category and type
  pages show before paginating. Defaults to **10**. Listings use their own
  **Number of jobs** instead.

## Appearance

![The Appearance settings tab](/docs/images/settings-appearance.jpg)

Six colors style every JobPress page and listing. Click **Select Color** to pick
one:

- **Primary/Branding Color** - buttons, links, and highlights.
- **Heading Title Color** - job titles and headings.
- **Content/Descriptions Color** - the job description on the
  [job page](/docs/single-job).
- **Secondary Color** - job details such as the type and deadline.
- **Border Color** - card and field borders.
- **Hover Color** - buttons and links on hover.

A [block](/docs/block#styles) or [Elementor widget](/docs/elementor#style-tab)
can override these colors for one listing, and the
[shortcode](/docs/shortcode#design-and-colors) takes them as attributes.

## Shortcodes

![The Shortcodes settings tab](/docs/images/settings-shortcodes.jpg)

The top of the tab, **Available Shortcode**, is a quick reference for the
`[jobpress]` shortcode and its attributes. The full guide is on the
[Shortcode](/docs/shortcode) page.

**Select Design** sets the [design](/docs/listing-designs) of every listing that
doesn't choose its own, from **Design V1: list** (the default) to
**Design V5: grid** - the same names the block and the Elementor widget use.

### Listing Defaults

What every listing shows unless the shortcode, block, or widget says otherwise.
Leave a text field empty to use the built-in text shown in grey.

- **Title** - default "Job openings". **Show the title**.
- **Subtitle** - default "Find the right job for you no matter what it is that
  you do." **Show the subtitle**.
- **Show the open positions count**
- **Show the job category**, **Show the job type**, **Show the location**,
  **Show the experience**, **Show the vacancies**,
  **Show the application deadline** - the details on each job card.
- **Apply button text** - default "Apply".
- **Show a search bar that opens the Jobs Page results** - off by default.
- **Show a "View all jobs" link to the Jobs Page** - off by default, with its
  **"View all jobs" link text**.

Settings marked **(also on the jobs archive)** - the job type, experience,
vacancies, deadline, and button text - also apply to the
[Jobs Page](/docs/jobs-page#job-cards) cards.

<Callout title="Tip">
Set your listing text and colors here first, then leave the block, widget, and
shortcode options on Default. Every listing on your site then changes together
when you update one setting.
</Callout>
