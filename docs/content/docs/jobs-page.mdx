The **Jobs Page** is the main careers page of your site: every open job, with
search and filters at the top. JobPress creates it on activation as
**Jobs Listing** (`/jobs-listing/`). Add it to your site menu as "Careers" or
"Jobs".

![The Jobs Page](/docs/images/jobs-page.jpg)

## Search and filters

The bar at the top has three parts that work together:

- **Search by job title or description** - finds jobs by keyword.
- **Select category** - shows one [job category](/docs/categories-and-types).
- **Select type** - shows one job type.

Click **Find Jobs** to see the results.

![Search results for "engineer" in Engineering](/docs/images/jobs-page-search.jpg)

When nothing matches, visitors see **No jobs found** and can change their
search.

![No jobs found](/docs/images/jobs-page-empty.jpg)

## Job cards

Each card shows the job title, **Job Type**, **Vacancies**, **Deadline**,
**Experience**, and an **Apply** button that opens the
[job page](/docs/single-job). Hide any of these details, or change the button
text, under
[Settings → Shortcodes → Listing Defaults](/docs/settings#listing-defaults).
The options marked *(also on the jobs archive)* apply here.

The page shows 10 jobs at a time, with page numbers underneath. Change the
number with **Jobs Per Page** in [Settings → General](/docs/settings#general).

## Choosing a different page

Pick any page under **Jobs Page** in
[Settings → General](/docs/settings#general). The page keeps its own title, but
JobPress replaces its content with the jobs layout above, so the page's own
content isn't shown.

<Callout title="Listings elsewhere point here">
A [shortcode](/docs/shortcode), [block](/docs/block), or
[Elementor widget](/docs/elementor) listing can show a search bar and a
**View all jobs** link. Both open the Jobs Page, so it is the one place
visitors search every opening.
</Callout>
