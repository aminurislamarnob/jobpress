If you build pages with Elementor, the **JobPress Jobs** widget gives you the
same listing as the [`[jobpress]` shortcode](/docs/jobpress/shortcode), with Elementor's
own style controls. It appears when Elementor is active.

![The JobPress Jobs widget in Elementor](/docs/jobpress/images/elementor-widget.jpg)

## Adding the widget

In the Elementor editor, search the widget panel for **JobPress Jobs** and drag
it onto the page. It shows your jobs right away, in your global design.

## Content tab

Every setting left on **Default** (or empty) follows the global
[Listing Defaults](/docs/jobpress/settings#listing-defaults), so changing those later
still updates the widget. Show/hide options offer **Default**, **Show**, and
**Hide**.

- **Layout** - **Design** and, for grids, **Columns**.
- **Header** - the title, subtitle, and open positions count.
- **Job Card** - which details each card shows, and the button text.
- **Search & Links** - a search bar that opens the
  [Jobs Page](/docs/jobpress/jobs-page) results, and a "View all jobs" link.
- **Query** - **Number of jobs**, **Categories**, **Job types**,
  **Only these jobs**, **Exclude jobs**, **Order by**, and **Order**.

Options that the chosen design doesn't display are hidden.

## Style tab

Style each part of the listing for this widget:

- **Colors** - the six JobPress colors, overriding
  [Settings → Appearance](/docs/jobpress/settings#appearance).
- **Header** - alignment, space below, and the title, subtitle, and count
  styles.
- **Job Card** - padding, space between cards, border radius, and background
  and border colors for the normal and hover states.
- **Job Title** and **Job Details** - colors, hover color, typography, and
  spacing.
- **Apply Button** - text and background colors, padding, border radius, and
  typography.
- **Category Group Header** (Designs V2 and V4) - background, the category name,
  description, and the **Openings badge**.
- **Search Bar** and **View All Jobs Link** - their colors, alignment, and
  spacing.

The usual **Advanced** tab options (margin, padding, visibility…) apply too.

<Callout title="Shortcode, block, or widget?">
All three show the same listing with the same options and defaults. Use the
widget in Elementor, the [block](/docs/jobpress/block) in the block editor, and the
[shortcode](/docs/jobpress/shortcode) anywhere else.
</Callout>
