The **JobPress Jobs** block is the block-editor version of the
[`[jobpress]` shortcode](/docs/jobpress/shortcode): the same listing and options, with a
live preview. It needs WordPress 6.3 or later.

![The JobPress Jobs block in the editor](/docs/jobpress/images/block-editor.jpg)

## Adding the block

In the block editor, click **+**, search for **jobs**, and pick
**JobPress Jobs**. The block shows your listing straight away, in your global
design. With no published jobs yet, it shows "No jobs yet. Your published jobs
will be listed here." and an **Add job** link.

## Settings

Select the block and open the **Settings** tab (gear icon) in the sidebar.
Anything left on **Default** follows the global
[Listing Defaults](/docs/jobpress/settings#listing-defaults), and the option shows which
value that is, such as **Default (Show)**. The **Listing Defaults** link opens
those settings.

- **Layout** - **Design** and, for Design V5, **Columns** (the most cards per
  row).
- **Header** - show or hide the **Title**, **Subtitle**, and
  **Open positions count**, and override the **Title text** and
  **Subtitle text**.
- **Job Card** - show or hide the **Category**, **Job type**, **Location**,
  **Experience**, **Vacancies**, and **Deadline**, and set the
  **Button text**. Only the details the chosen design displays are listed.
- **Search & Links** - a **Search bar** that opens the
  [Jobs Page](/docs/jobpress/jobs-page) results, and a **"View all jobs" link** with its
  **Link text**.
- **Query** - which jobs to list: **Number of jobs**, **Categories**,
  **Job types**, **Only these jobs**, **Leave out these jobs**, **Order by**
  (Date, Title, Menu order, Random), and **Order** (Descending, Ascending).

## Styles

The **Styles** tab (half-circle icon) styles this block only:

- **Listing colors** - **Brand**, **Hover**, **Headings**, **Secondary text**,
  **Content text**, and **Borders**. Unset colors use
  [Settings → Appearance](/docs/jobpress/settings#appearance).
- **Job cards** - **Space between cards (px)**, **Padding (px)**, and
  **Border radius (px)**.
- **Card colors** - **Card background**, **Button text**,
  **Button background**, **Button text on hover**, and
  **Button background on hover**.

The usual block options apply too: wide and full width in the toolbar, and
margin, padding, and background in the sidebar.

## Converting from a shortcode

Already using `[jobpress]`? Select the Shortcode block and choose
**Transform to → JobPress Jobs**, or paste a `[jobpress]` shortcode into an
empty paragraph. Its attributes become block settings. The block transforms back
to a shortcode the same way, keeping everything except the Styles tab options.

<Callout title="Older WordPress">
On WordPress versions before 6.3 the block isn't available - use the
[shortcode](/docs/jobpress/shortcode), which shows the same listing.
</Callout>
