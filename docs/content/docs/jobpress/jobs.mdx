Every opening lives under **JobPress** in the wp-admin menu. Jobs work like
WordPress posts: write the description in the editor, then fill in the job
details underneath.

## The jobs list

![The jobs list](/docs/jobpress/images/jobs-list.jpg)

**JobPress → All Jobs** lists every job.

| Column | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | The job title - click it to edit |
| **Author** | Who posted the job |
| **Job Category** | The job's [categories](/docs/jobpress/categories-and-types) |
| **Job Type** | The job's [types](/docs/jobpress/categories-and-types) |
| **Date** | Published, scheduled, or last modified date |

**Search Jobs** finds a job by title or description, and **All dates** filters by
month. Hover a row for **Edit**, **Quick Edit**, **Trash**, and **View**.
**Bulk actions** edits or trashes several jobs at once.

## Adding a job

![The job editor](/docs/jobpress/images/job-edit.jpg)

Click **Add New Job** and:

1. Enter the job title and write the description: the role, what the person
   will do, what you are looking for, and what you offer.
2. In the sidebar, tick the job's **Job Categories** and **Job Types**.
3. Optionally set a cover image and an excerpt (see below).
4. Fill in **Job Others Informations** under the editor.
5. Click **Publish**.

The job appears on your [Jobs Page](/docs/jobpress/jobs-page) and in every
[listing](/docs/jobpress/listing-designs) straight away. Unpublish or trash it when the
position is filled.

### Cover image and excerpt

- **Cover image** (the featured image) - shown at the top of the
  [single job page](/docs/jobpress/single-job).
- **Excerpt** - a one-line summary shown on the cards of
  [Design V5](/docs/jobpress/listing-designs#design-v5-grid).

## Job details

![The Job Others Informations box](/docs/jobpress/images/job-fields.png)

The **Job Others Informations** box holds the details candidates scan first.
Every field is optional, and empty fields are hidden on your site.

- **Job Vacancy** - how many people you are hiring.
- **Experience** - free text, such as `3+ years` or `No experience required`.
- **Working Hours** - such as `9:00 AM - 6:00 PM`.
- **Working Days** - such as `Sunday - Thursday`.
- **Salary** - free text, so you can write a range, a currency, and a period.
- **Application Deadline** - a date, shown in your site's date format.
- **Job Location** - a city, an address, or `Remote`.
- **Google Map iFrame Embed Code** - in Google Maps, choose
  **Share → Embed a map**, copy the HTML, and paste it here. The map shows
  under the job description. Other embed codes are removed.

Where each detail appears:

| Detail | Shown on |
| --- | --- |
| Location | The job page, Designs V1 and V2 |
| Experience | The job page, the Jobs Page, Designs V2, V3, and V4 |
| Vacancy and deadline | The job page, the Jobs Page, Designs V3 and V4 |
| Working hours, working days, salary | The job page |

You can hide a detail everywhere from
[Listing Defaults](/docs/jobpress/settings#listing-defaults), or per listing.

## How candidates apply

**Job Application Collect Medium** decides what the apply section at the bottom
of the job page shows:

- **Collect application through email.** - shows the text you write in
  **Resume Submit Description With Email Address**, for example "Send your CV to
  careers@yourcompany.com". Email addresses become clickable links.
- **Collect application through form.** - shows a Contact Form 7 form. Pick it
  under **Select Contact Form 7 (By this form candidate can apply to this
  job.)**. This option is disabled until the Contact Form 7 plugin is active.
- **Default (That you inserted on JobPress settings page.)** - shows the
  **Resume Submit Instruction** from
  [Settings → General](/docs/jobpress/settings#general), so you can write it once for
  every job.

<Callout title="Tip">
Most jobs share one way to apply. Set the **Resume Submit Instruction** once in
Settings and leave each job on **Default** - you then only touch this box for
jobs that apply differently.
</Callout>
