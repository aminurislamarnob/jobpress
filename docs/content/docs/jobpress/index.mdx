Welcome to the **JobPress** documentation. JobPress is a free WordPress plugin
that adds a careers section to your own website: post your openings from
wp-admin, and visitors browse, search, and apply on a ready-made jobs page.

![The JobPress jobs page](/docs/jobpress/images/jobs-page.jpg)

## What you get

- **[Jobs](/docs/jobpress/jobs)** - post openings like WordPress posts, with vacancy,
  experience, salary, working hours, deadline, location, and a Google map.
- **[Categories and types](/docs/jobpress/categories-and-types)** - group jobs by team
  (Engineering, Design…) and by type (Full Time, Remote…).
- **[Jobs Page](/docs/jobpress/jobs-page)** - a public jobs page, created for you, with
  search and category and type filters.
- **[Single job page](/docs/jobpress/single-job)** - a job summary sidebar and an apply
  section, by email or with a Contact Form 7 form.
- **[Listing designs](/docs/jobpress/listing-designs)** - five layouts, from a compact
  list to a card grid, and you can mix them on one page.
- **[Shortcode](/docs/jobpress/shortcode)** - show a job listing anywhere with
  `[jobpress]`, filtered by category, type, or hand-picked jobs.
- **[JobPress Jobs block](/docs/jobpress/block)** - the same listing in the block editor,
  with a live preview and style controls.
- **[Elementor widget](/docs/jobpress/elementor)** - the same listing in Elementor, with
  full Style tab controls.
- **[Settings](/docs/jobpress/settings)** - your jobs page, application text, brand
  colors, and what every listing shows by default.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Version |
| --- | --- |
| WordPress | 5.6 or higher (tested up to 7.1) |
| PHP | 7.4 or higher |
| JobPress Jobs block | WordPress 6.3 or higher |
| Application forms | [Contact Form 7](https://wordpress.org/plugins/contact-form-7/) (optional) |
| Elementor widget | [Elementor](https://wordpress.org/plugins/elementor/) (optional) |

JobPress works with classic and block themes, such as Twenty Twenty-Five.
