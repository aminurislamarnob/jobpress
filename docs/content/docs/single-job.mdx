Every job gets its own page at `/job/<job-name>/`. Candidates reach it from the
**Apply** button on the [Jobs Page](/docs/jobs-page) or any
[listing](/docs/listing-designs).

![A single job page](/docs/images/single-job.jpg)

## The page layout

- **Title and cover image** - the job title and the cover (featured) image, if
  you set one.
- **Description** - everything you wrote in the editor.
- **Map** - the Google map from **Google Map iFrame Embed Code**, under the
  description.
- **Job Summary** - a sidebar with **Location**, **Job Type**,
  **Application Deadline**, **Experience**, **Working Days**,
  **Working Hours**, **Salary**, and **Vacancy**. Details you left empty are
  left out.
- **Apply Now** - the sidebar button jumps to the apply section.

The sidebar sits on the right. To move it to the left, tick
**Single Page Sidebar Position** in
[Settings → General](/docs/settings#general).

## The apply section

![The apply section with a Contact Form 7 form](/docs/images/single-job-apply.jpg)

The bottom of the page is headed **Apply for The Position:** followed by the job
title. What it shows depends on the job's
[Job Application Collect Medium](/docs/jobs#how-candidates-apply):

- a Contact Form 7 form, as above
- your email instructions for this job
- the **Resume Submit Instruction** from
  [Settings → General](/docs/settings#general)

A job with no medium chosen uses the **Resume Submit Instruction** too. If that
is also empty, candidates see "No application process added by the
recruiter.", so set it once to make sure every job has a way to apply.

<Callout title="Receiving applications">
Contact Form 7 sends each application to the email address set in that form's
**Mail** tab. Add a file upload field to the form to collect CVs.
</Callout>

## Colors

The page uses your brand colors from
[Settings → Appearance](/docs/settings#appearance): the **Apply Now** button,
links, headings, and borders.
