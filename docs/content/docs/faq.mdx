## Is JobPress free?

Yes. JobPress is free and open source on WordPress.org, including all five
listing designs, the block, and the Elementor widget.

## What are the requirements?

WordPress 5.6+ and PHP 7.4+. The [JobPress Jobs block](/docs/block) needs
WordPress 6.3+. Tested up to WordPress 7.1.

## How do candidates apply?

By email or with a form. Each job either shows your email instructions or a
[Contact Form 7](https://wordpress.org/plugins/contact-form-7/) form, or falls
back to the **Resume Submit Instruction** in Settings. Contact Form 7 is the
only form plugin supported for now. See
[How candidates apply](/docs/jobs#how-candidates-apply).

## Where do applications go?

JobPress doesn't store applications. With email instructions, candidates email
you directly. With a Contact Form 7 form, the form sends each application to the
address in its **Mail** tab.

## Does it work with my theme?

JobPress works with classic and block themes. On block themes such as Twenty
Twenty-Five, job pages show the theme's header and footer. Developers can
override JobPress templates by copying them into a `jobpress` folder in the
theme.

## Should I use the block, the shortcode, or the Elementor widget?

They show the same listing with the same options and defaults. Use the
[block](/docs/block) in the block editor, the [widget](/docs/elementor) in
Elementor, and the [shortcode](/docs/shortcode) anywhere else.

## Can I show only some jobs on a page?

Yes. Every listing can filter by category, job type, or hand-picked jobs, and
limit and order them. See [Choosing the jobs](/docs/shortcode#choosing-the-jobs).

## Can I use different designs on one page?

Yes. Each listing can pick its own [design](/docs/listing-designs), and
listings with different designs can share a page.

## What happens to my jobs if I delete the plugin?

Deleting JobPress removes its settings but keeps your jobs, categories, types,
and the Jobs Page, so reinstalling brings everything back. To remove those too,
add `define( 'JOBPRESS_REMOVE_ALL_DATA', true );` to `wp-config.php` before
deleting the plugin.

## My job pages return 404s. What do I do?

Go to **Settings → Permalinks** and click **Save Changes** to refresh the URLs.
